⚖️ Legal, Permits & Compliance — Property Ecosystem SuperApp
📝 Compliance Areas & Action Plan
Compliance Area
Action Plan
RERA Compliance (Real Estate Regulation Act)
Ensure listing properties are RERA registered; onboarding process to verify RERA number for builders & projects
Service Provider Agreements
Legal MoUs with plumbers, electricians, contractors, interior vendors, material suppliers ensuring service standards & liability clauses
KYC & Identity Verification
Implement e-KYC for Dealers, Builders, Service Providers, and Material Vendors through Aadhar/Company PAN verification
Document Handling & Data Protection
Compliance with IT Act, 2000 and GDPR-like principles for document handling, storage, and user privacy policies
Legal Document Workflow (Sale Deed, Registry)
Partner with authorized Legal Agents/Law Firms for documentation, e-Sign workflow compliance, and Stamp Duty procedures
Payment & Refund Policies
Transparent online payment structure with clearly defined refund & cancellation terms for services & transactions
Labour Law Compliance
Ensure service partners comply with local labour regulations (minimum wage, working conditions, etc.)
Consumer Protection Act Compliance
Ensure transparent service policies, fair trade practices, and grievance redressal mechanism
GST Compliance & Invoicing
All service and product transactions invoiced with GST, automated through payment gateway integrations
E-commerce Legal Compliance (Marketplace Model)
Ensure proper Vendor Agreements, Return Policies, and TCS deductions on material sales (Section 194-O of IT Act)


Key Legal & Operational Priorities:
RERA Compliance Workflow — No listing without RERA verification.
Legal Documentation Flow — Digitally enabled RC Transfer, Registry, and Stamp Duty payments.
Vendor Contracts & QC Audits — Legal agreements + Field Inspections.
Multi-Layer Customer Support Setup — Phone, WhatsApp, App Chat for frictionless support.

📝 Key Insights from SWOT across Segments:
No single platform offers an end-to-end ecosystem — The biggest gap.
Most players are specialized (only listings, only services, only interiors) — There’s a consolidation opportunity.
Organizing Local Vendors & Service Partners is a massive opportunity — Hyperlocal advantage.
Legal & Documentation services are ignored by all major players — A huge pain point to solve.
Subscription-based AMC models are still untapped in property services — Potential recurring revenue.
